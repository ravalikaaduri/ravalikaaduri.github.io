A little about me

People make the world interesting.

I like people. I like the stories they carry, the places they come from, the way they see the world. Put me in a room with people I’ve never met and, chances are, I’ll want to know who they are.

I care about doing what I say I’ll do. I care about being kind when nobody is keeping score. I care about the small things too — checking in, making time, noticing when someone needs a little help.

The world is already moving fast. I don’t need to make it faster. I’m ambitious, absolutely. I want to grow, build, learn, and do meaningful work. But I also want sunlight through the window, a good conversation that runs too long, a little room to think, and time for the people I love.
